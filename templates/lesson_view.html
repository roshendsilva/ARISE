The Covenantal Assembly (Ekklesia)

From Mount Sinai's Old Covenant to Christ's New Covenant Assembly upon Saint Peter (Matthew 16:18).

Mount Sinai's Old Covenant assembly fulfilled in Christ's New Covenant Catholic Church (Matthew 16:18).

2. The Mystical Body of Christ

Christ as Divine Head, baptized believers as interconnected living limbs, animated by the Holy Spirit (1 Cor 12:27).
