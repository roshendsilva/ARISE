Mission & Vision

In an era often dominated by superficial debates and theological misunderstandings, ARISE elevates apologetics back to its historic dignity: integrating biblical exegesis, patristic archaeology, and magisterial documents to demonstrate the truth, beauty, and coherence of the Catholic Faith.

Whether examining Marian dogmas, Papal primacy, Sacramental theology, or Sola Scriptura, ARISE answers objections with charitable clarity while serving as an open repository for all seekers, scholars, Orthodox, secular, and non-Catholic inquirers.

Content Integrity Standard

  • Verified Primary Sources: All citations from Scripture, Church Fathers (AD 90–800), and Ecumenical Councils are verified against authentic primary texts.
  • Respectful External Dialogue: We present all non-Catholic, Orthodox, secular, and Protestant perspectives accurately without caricature or strawman arguments.
  • Unabridged 8-Part Analytical Format: Every article follows a structured sequence: Overview, Objections, Biblical Proofs, Patristic Evidence, Magisterial Teaching, Philosophical Logic, Refutations, and Practical Takeaways.

Key Theological Concepts

Latria

Supreme divine worship offered exclusively to God (Father, Son, and Holy Spirit).

Dulia

Honor and veneration accorded to holy angels and saints as God's faithful servants.

Hyperdulia

Highest creaturely veneration uniquely accorded to Mary as Mother of God (Theotokos).
