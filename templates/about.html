About ARISE

Apologetics for the Reasoned Interpretation of Scripture & Explanation

Our Mission & Vision

ARISE was founded to provide Christians, seekers, and scholars with an uncompromised, authentic, and scholarly Catholic apologetics repository.

In an age dominated by soundbites and misrepresentations, ARISE elevates apologetics back to its historic dignity: combining biblical exegesis, patristic archaeology, and magisterial documents to defend the beauty and truth of the Catholic Faith.

Content Integrity Standard

Every article on ARISE adheres to strict scholarly guidelines:

  • No Fabricated Sources: All citations from Scripture, Church Fathers, and Councils are verified against historical manuscripts and published translations.
  • Respectful Ecumenical & Philosophical Tone: We present all non-Catholic, Orthodox, secular, and inquirer positions fairly without caricature, answering objections with charitable clarity.
  • Rigorous 8-Part Structure: Every defense follows a uniform analytical sequence to ensure complete thoroughness.

Theological Terminology Glossary

Latria

Supreme Divine Worship due to Almighty God alone (Father, Son, Holy Spirit).

Dulia

Veneration and honor paid to holy angels and human saints.

Hyperdulia

Highest creaturely veneration given uniquely to the Blessed Virgin Mary as Mother of God.
